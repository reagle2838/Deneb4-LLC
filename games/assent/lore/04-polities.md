# IV. The Polities of 2071

Twenty human polities will cast Assent at the Convocation, each weighted by
the square root of its population. That's the Penrose compromise, written into
the Accord so the largest polities matter most without deciding everything
alone. Borders have moved since the 2020s: climate migration, the Seven
Weeks and fifty years of AI-driven economics redrew much of the map. What
hasn't changed is that people still want to be free, safe, fed, remembered
and understood, in different proportions.

Each polity has hidden **values** on the three axes. *Listen* reveals them,
and so does Ananke's *Forecast*. Before that you only see the public
temperament below.

## How polities think

- **Values** decide whose honest arguments land.
- **Technology** (0–1) decides who can use what. Verdance struggles in
  highly technological polities, and Ananke's gifts impress everywhere.
- **Openness** (0–1) is how movable a polity is at all. The Atlantic Seaboard
  is hard to move. The Sahel Compact is young and open.
- **Dependence** builds up from gifts. Past 0.6 a polity may rise against the
  **Gilded Cage**.
- **Suspicion** builds up hidden from Whispers. The Witness samples it every
  Epoch.
- **Sovereignty** is whatever Assent belongs to no god.
